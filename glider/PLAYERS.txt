STARQLIDER 1.0                                              2026-10-31
==============

A first-person wireframe hovercraft raid for the Sinclair QL, written
for a stock machine: 68008 at 7.5 MHz, 128K, mode 4. Developed for the
Sinclair QL game jam of the Retro Programmers United for Obsolete
Systems (RPUFOS), Halloween 2026:

  https://itch.io/jam/rpufos-sinclair-ql


RUNNING IT

- Q-emuLator (Windows, Mac): open starqlider.qlpak. The package sets
  up a 128K QL at the original speed and boots the game.
- A real QL, a vDriveQL, the ZX Spectrum Next or MiSTer QL cores, or
  another emulator: starqlider.mdv is a Microdrive image. Put it in
  mdv1 and boot (the boot file runs mdv1_glider_bin).

The game takes the whole machine over: to quit, reset it.


THE GAME

Destroy every white crystal in the sector. The crystals launch red
enemy gliders that defend them, and from sector 2 on the crystals fire
at you too -- from further than your own guns reach, so attack them on
the move: slide across their line of fire, and use the towers as
cover. The radar at the top shows the gliders and mines in red and the
crystals in white; crystals out of radar range sit on its rim, in
their direction. The count of crystals left is at the top right, next
to the score.

When the last crystal goes, you warp to the next sector. Each of the
first eight adds something new, named on its card; after the eighth
they come round again, harder, and keep coming. Your shield (the bar
at the top left) refills at each warp, less on the way round. Bumping
into things, mines and enemy fire wear it down; at zero the windshield
cracks and you lose a craft. Three craft per game (the spares are the
small deltas under the shield bar).


CONTROLS

  Left / Right   turn
  Up             thrust
  Down           brake; keep it held at a standstill to reverse
  Space          fire (hold it down for autofire)
  Esc            pause

A joystick in CTL1 plays the whole game (it is the arrows and Space).
The craft drifts: velocity lags the heading, so turning at speed
slides you sideways. That is the game's feel, not a bug.


SCORING

  mine 50, dart 100, wedge 200, kite 300, crystal 1000,
  and 1000 x the sector number for clearing a sector.


KNOWN ISSUES

- Busy scenes drop to 10-12 frames a second on a stock QL.
- Enemy gliders fly through obstacles and through each other.
- On the MiSTer QL core the wireframes flicker: a core bug, reported
  upstream.
- The high score is not saved (the game runs without the QL's
  operating system).
